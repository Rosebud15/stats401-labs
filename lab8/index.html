6. Design Description

I treated each meaningful paragraph or short policy block as one document unit while preserving its chapter, section, subsection, page number, and original text. The preprocessing script removes table-of-contents pages, page numbers, very short fragments, and duplicate passages. I generated semantic vectors with the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 SentenceTransformer model. I then reduced those vectors to two dimensions with UMAP using 15 neighbors, a minimum distance of 0.15, cosine distance, and random state 401. KMeans clustering was applied to the original high-dimensional embedding vectors with eight clusters. Each cluster is summarized with its highest TF-IDF terms and representative passages in the generated cluster report, and the displayed topic names are constructed directly from those characteristic terms.

In the semantic map, position represents the UMAP projection, color represents semantic topic, and point size represents passage length. Search and section/topic filters make it possible to compare formal document structure with semantic structure, while zoom and pan support closer inspection of dense regions. Clicking a passage keeps its details visible and highlights five nearest neighbors calculated from the original embedding space rather than from the 2D map. The Topic × Section matrix summarizes where each semantic topic occurs in the bulletin's formal organization. The two views are coordinated: selecting a passage highlights its matrix cell, and selecting a matrix cell highlights the corresponding passages on the map. Together, these interactions reveal themes that cross section boundaries and help distinguish structural proximity from semantic similarity.
