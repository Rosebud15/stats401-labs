Network Analysis

The visualization reveals several changes in the commercial network across the 60-day period.

1. How does overall network connectivity change across the 60 days?

Overall, the network becomes more connected and commercially active over time. The earlier days tend to show fewer active relationships and more fragmented activity, while the later days contain more simultaneous transactions and broader participation across the network. This change becomes especially noticeable during the final third of the period.

2. Which companies become more central or commercially active over time?

Several companies become increasingly important as the network develops. In particular, Ion Systems and Cedar Retail become more central because they begin interacting with a wider range of companies and participate in more commercial activity. Apex Textiles also becomes more active later in the period. Other companies, such as Fusion Electronics, remain commercially important but do not necessarily become more structurally central because their activity is concentrated among a smaller number of partners.

3. Are there periods when the network consists of relatively separate clusters?

Yes. The network is generally more fragmented during the earlier days, when companies often form several small and relatively separate groups. As time progresses, these groups become more interconnected. However, the clusters are mostly temporary rather than permanent. Companies that appear separated on an individual day are often connected through transactions occurring on nearby days.

4. Which commercial relationships appear, disappear, or become stronger over time?

The network shows considerable turnover in commercial relationships. Some relationships are active mainly during the beginning of the period and later disappear, while others emerge during the middle or final portion of the timeline. The later period introduces several new relationships involving companies such as Ion Systems and Cedar Retail, helping explain their growing importance in the network. Some existing relationships also become stronger through more frequent or higher-value transactions.

5. Does the network become more or less cross-regional over time?

The network becomes increasingly cross-regional over time. Earlier activity includes a mixture of within-region and cross-region transactions, while later activity is more strongly dominated by connections between companies located in different regions. This suggests that the commercial network becomes more geographically integrated as the 60-day period progresses.
